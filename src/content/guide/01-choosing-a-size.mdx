The first thing to decide when building your own TaTaCon is how big you want it.

<Callout type="note">
  Personally, I made my big one 18 inches in diameter and my small one 12 inches.
</Callout>

The official arcade drums are about 16-18 inches, but you can make this whatever size you want.

---

## Getting the Wood

Once you decide on what size you want, you will have to get some wood that can cut into **two** circles of your chosen diameter. One will be used later for the Dons and Kas while the other will act as a backplate.

The two circles do not need to fit on one piece of wood — you can get two pieces of wood as long as they can both cut to a whole circle of your chosen diameter.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you want a 12 inch diameter TaTaCon, you will need to get at least a 12x24 inch panel of wood or two 12x12 inch panels.
</Callout>

## Wood Thickness

Additionally, you will have to decide on the thickness of the wood.

<Callout type="note">
  On my big TaTaCon, I used 1/2" thick wood while on the small TaTaCon, I used 1/4" thick wood.
</Callout>

My recommendation is to use **1/4" for anything 12 inches or smaller** and **1/2" for anything bigger**. This may impact the cost, so if you want to use 1/4" go for it — I wouldn't go any thinner though unless you hit really really lightly.

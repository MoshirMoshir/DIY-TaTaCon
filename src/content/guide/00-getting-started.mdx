Alright, you've decided to create your own TaTaCon instead of buying a Hori or one of those knockoffs. Here's what you need to know before you begin.

## Before You Start

<Callout type="note">
  If you are not familiar or a beginner with tech/engineering, this shouldn't be too difficult, but will still require a bit of effort. If you are comfortable with electronics/engineering, this should be a breeze — feel free to make modifications as needed!
</Callout>

- This will probably cost you a little more than a Hori
- Soldering skills are optional, but would help tremendously
- You will need access to a computer with Windows, Linux, or macOS

## Required Tools

If you are unable to get easy access to these tools (or it's too expensive to rent), you're better off buying a TaTaCon.

- **Electric Jigsaw** — you could use a handsaw but a powered jigsaw is preferred
- **Drill with drill bits**
- **Scissors**
- **Ruler**
- **Soldering kit** *(optional)*
- **Wire cutters** *(optional)*

<Callout type="warning">
  If you do not feel comfortable handling any of the above tools, you're better off buying a TaTaCon.
</Callout>

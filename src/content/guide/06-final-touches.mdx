You're almost done fully building the TaTaCon! The last two things are the **stand** and the **cover**.

## Drum Surface

Although I have heard of ways of getting official drum skin, I recommend **gum rubber** — it's the same material used on practice drum pads. You can purchase them on Amazon, and then just cut them to size. I recommend **1/4" thickness**, but 1/8" works too.

Once you cut them, you can just glue them on. You can use superglue, epoxy, whatever glue that works with rubber and wood.

## Stand Options

You can either cut out wood that elevates and angles the drum accordingly, or, if the TaTaCon is small, you can just order a laptop stand — then it's also adjustable!
